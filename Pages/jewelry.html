Intricate woven surfaces of elegant earrings, beads, bracelets, pins, and rings sparkle with light. Open weaves shimmer with brilliance and light reflecting from mirror polished interiors. These beautiful works of art are loved and treasured.

Twisted, wrapped, flat and round 18k white, yellow, green and rose gold wires are fabricated in his studio prior to weaving. His traditional twill weaves of one hundred gold wires to the inch are historically unique.

Woven on looms that Stuart has designed and built, the gold fabric is painstakingly produced in an infinite variety of patterns. After the weaving is cut from the loom, it is meticulously soldered, sawed and then formed into elaborate patchwork creations constructed using tiny pieces of weaving and 18k sheet gold.
